ZSKY 2003 reads it as a name, and explains it as a delivery receipt. The same notations occur on 173 and 367. There are similar examples with the names Mao 卯 (23, 60, 146, 318, 396) and Ding 鼎 (249; on the verso side is the notation “30”). These three words only occur on the recto sides of turtle plastrons and are not written close to any divination cracks.
