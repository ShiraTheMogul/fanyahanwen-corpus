Divined on Jiyou: On the next day, Geng[xu], our lord will call the Many Servitors to banquet and see (or: have audience about/with) His Highness. Used. Not all.112 1 110 Sun Yabing (2011a: 111, n.6) emends 延after 祖甲. 111 大 here is an adverb. The same usage occurs in da sui 大歲(劌)/xiao sui 小歲(劌) at 228.2-3.
