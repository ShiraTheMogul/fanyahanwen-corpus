On Yiyou, sacrifice... 283 The ten cracks made about this divination statement is the highest count in the entire corpus. (HYZ 310, a 9 cm x 7 cm scapula fragment, has a divination account with two cracks numbered 9 and 10.) The shell has a small hole-shaped fracture in between the words 一 “one” and 巛 “calamity”, and part of 巛has been nicked off. The ZSKY editors suggest that only a single graph is missing, and based on context the missing graph would likely have been the negative wang 亡 “not have”. HYZ 178 | 195
