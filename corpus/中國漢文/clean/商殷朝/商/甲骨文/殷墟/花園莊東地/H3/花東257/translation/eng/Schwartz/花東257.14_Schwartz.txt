[Used]. 1 387 The graph is composed of fu 阜 “hill” (classifier) + yi 矢 “arrow” (phonetic?). The reading of this graph is uncertain and I am not sure if it should be read as the name of a person or as a grammatical word in combination with 告 “announce”. A person with this name is recorded in period I OBI, and HJ 926r records a person called Zi Yi 子 “lord Yi”. See Jao Tsung-I 1959: 529, 256 | HYZ 257
