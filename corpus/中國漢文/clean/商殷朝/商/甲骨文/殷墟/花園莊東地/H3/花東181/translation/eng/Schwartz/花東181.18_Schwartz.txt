Divined on Ji: It should be stew.299 1 299 This word also occurs on 183 and there it takes Ancestress Ding as its object. Wang Guowei (cited in Gulin #3209) reads it shu 餗. Takashima (2010: I.786) translates it as “(food) offering”. Gao Heng’s commentary (2010: 315) to the Yijing line statement fu gong shu 覆公餗 “Overturn the duke’s stew” (Hexagram “Ding”鼎) says “vegetables in broth and porridge can all be called 餗.” HYZ 181 | 201
