# WORK_BASE_TITLE: 花园庄东地甲骨
# NATION: 商殷朝
# CATEGORIES: 商殷朝，甲骨文
# CATALOG: HYZ 395+548.9
# SOURCE: Schwartz, A. C. (2019). The Oracle Bone Inscriptions from Huayuanzhuang East. De Gruyter. https://doi.org/10.1515/9781501505294-001

Divined on Guiyou: Our lord is likely to make a capture. Our lord read the crack and said, “Should capture (something).” Used. Four musk deer, six rabbits.
