On Guisi, [sacrifice] (to) Gui Child one ewe. 1 (猷 > you 猶) “plan” in Western Zhou literature occurs in combination with mou 謀: for instance, Shangshu, “Jun chen” 君陳: 爾有嘉謀嘉猷 “You have excellent plans and ideas”; and Hu zhong 鐘 (JC 260): 惟上帝百神保余小子，朕猷有成亡競 “It is G-d and the hundred spirits who protect me, the young child; my plans have success without contention.” 427 The reading of this graph as “father” is uncertain.
