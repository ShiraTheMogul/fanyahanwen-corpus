# WORK_BASE_TITLE: 花園莊東地甲骨
# NATION: 商殷朝
# CATEGORIES: 商殷朝，甲骨文
# CATALOG: HYZ 381.1
# SOURCE: Schwartz, A. C. (2019). The Oracle Bone Inscriptions from Huayuanzhuang East. De Gruyter. https://doi.org/10.1515/9781501505294-001

Divined on the evening of Wuxu: Tomorrow, day Ji, if our lord [pursues] from the north side of Wei and hunts strong-armed, (he) will make an encounter. Our lord read the crack and said, “There will not be three, but should be at least one or two. There will likely be racing horses.” Used. 1
