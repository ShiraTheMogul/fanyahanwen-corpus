Tested: (for) Sheng. 23 is composed of a standing man with an emphasized eye (= shi 視) + 首 “head”. The graph does not occur elsewhere in Shang oracle bone inscriptions. The phrase mian jian 面見 however does occur elsewhere in this corpus (195), and this leads me to suggest that it should be read as a ligature, either writing 面見 or 面視. HYZ 206 | 217
