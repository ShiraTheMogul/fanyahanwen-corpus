Divined on Dinghai: If lord Jin goes, there will be no calamities. 1 368 depicts a person 人 bound with rope. I take it in its original sense and read it as the noun, the object of the verb yi 肄; it is a verb that takes men of the enemy Shao territory as its object in divinations (8-9) later in this sequence. An alternative reading is to take it as a verb and in coordination with 肄. 369 “Shao” refers to men from the enemy Shao territory. 370 Hua is the same person called lord Hua 子 HYZ 249-HYZ 250 | 247
