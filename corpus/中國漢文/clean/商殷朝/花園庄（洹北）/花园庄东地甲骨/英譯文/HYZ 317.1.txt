Our lord tested. 1 472 I read “kill” and “cut” as coordinate verbs; the word-order and grammar is parallel with qi sha mao 其殺卯 “should kill and butcher” (223). 473 You ruo 友若 ought to be equivalent to 又(有)若 on 361. Exchange between 友and 又 occurs in OBI; see HJ 10196. The person called You You is written both as 由友and as 由又. HYZ 319 | 299
