Divined on Jiawu: Our lord will invite (His Highness), (but he) likely will not arrive. Our lord read the crack(s) and said, “If (he) does not come, (I) will call to feast.” Used. Carved (meat used for) Ancestor Jia’s Rong-rite. 12
