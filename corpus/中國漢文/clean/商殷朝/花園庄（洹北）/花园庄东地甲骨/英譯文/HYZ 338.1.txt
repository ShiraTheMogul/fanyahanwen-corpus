On Jiachen, sacrificing some mountain rams (to) Ancestor Jia, have (an- other) offering (> pair it with something else). Used. 1 set reveals Bi must have been injured on day Yimao (52/60) at practice for an upcoming music and dance performance later the same week. Divinations made on Bingchen (53/60), the next day, as well as on Gengshen (57/60), several days later (see HYZ 209), confirm it. HYZ 340 | 307
