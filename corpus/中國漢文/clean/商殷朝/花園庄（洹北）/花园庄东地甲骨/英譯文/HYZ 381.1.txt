Divined on the evening of Wuxu: Tomorrow, day Ji, if our lord [pursues] from the north side of Wei and hunts strong-armed, (he) will make an en- counter. Our lord read the crack and said, “There will not be three, but should be at least one or two. There will likely be racing horses.”515 Used. 1
