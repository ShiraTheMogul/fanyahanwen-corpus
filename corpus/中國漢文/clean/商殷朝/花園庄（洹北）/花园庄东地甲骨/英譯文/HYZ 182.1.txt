301 The preface in this divination account is 小 卜不吉，貞. The text before the word zhen 貞 “tested” is the result of the previous divinations (32)-(33). The diviner is testing why the previous divinations about sacrificing some small pen-raised sheep to Child Gui were unlucky. Prefaces in this format are rare (see HYZ 123 + Jiyi 561), and in this particular case the reason it was recorded might have had to do with orientation and the layout of the inscriptions, since the location of (35) is far removed from the divinations it intended to test on this densely in- scribed bone.
