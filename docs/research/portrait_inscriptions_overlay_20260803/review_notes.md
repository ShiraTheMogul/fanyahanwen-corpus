# Review notes

## Witness policy

The clean tree follows the actual painted or inscribed witness. A printed collection, museum summary, translation, or later quotation is used for collation, not as permission to replace what the object reads. Where witnesses differ, the difference belongs in metadata.

## 楊洪異文

The painting visibly includes `旌旗所指 犬羊遁藏` between `一騎前驅 萬夫莫當` and `知其內者...`, and reads `噫斯人也殆所謂`. The transmitted 四庫本 text omits the added couplet and `殆`. The clean transcription follows the painting; the collected-text readings are recorded as variants.

## 道元

The commonly circulated transcription of the second line is unreliable. The remaining lines are useful, but the work stays out of the clean tree until that line is checked against a better facsimile or an authoritative temple transcription.

## 果親王允禮

The Smithsonian publishes a full English translation and identifies the poem as the prince's self-inscription. That proves the unit is substantial Hanwen, but translation is not a substitute for a diplomatic Chinese transcription. The brush text therefore remains queued.

## 吉田松陰 variants

There are several self-inscribed portrait copies with different prose codas. The included image is the 吉田家本-type text beginning `己未五月我有關左之厄`; it must not be silently replaced by the better-known version beginning `己未五月吾執拘送關左`.

## Geographic correction: Oda Nobunaga portrait (item 2)

The Wikimedia filename attributes the image to the Hideyoshi Kiyomasa Memorial Hall in Nagoya, but its own source link resolves to the Kobe City Museum record. The work is therefore filed under 摂津国, not 尾張国. This is a concrete example of why source filenames must not decide the geographic layer.

## 豊国大明神肖像賛（item 1）

The 1721 praise is long enough that isolated readable phrases are dangerous: arranging them into plausible prose would silently manufacture a text. The review package therefore distinguishes a secure colophon from readable fragments and explicitly denies that their order has been settled.

## 高台寺本・西教寺本（items 3–4）

Portrait catalogues and web summaries frequently discuss several Hideyoshi portraits together. The high-level attribution or the ending of a praise is not enough to transfer a complete transcription between objects. The Kōdai-ji and Saikyō-ji images remain separate review witnesses; Saikyō-ji's multiple praises must also be segmented before ingestion.

## 聖徳太子像賛（item 16）

The four-line text is legible as `敬禮救世觀世音／傳燈東方粟散王／從於西方來誕生／開演妙法度衆生`. Later textual quotations sometimes offer different wording, but the overlay records this painted witness. The work is not promoted because its production/provenance region remains unknown; the depicted person's association with 大和国 is not sufficient filing evidence.

## Province/domain layer

For Japanese material, `period / historical province or domain / work` is required whenever the geographic layer can be supported. The basis should be recorded. Subject biography is a fallback only; it must not override a known production, dedication, institutional, or transmission context. Mori material tied to Aki belongs under `安芸国`.
