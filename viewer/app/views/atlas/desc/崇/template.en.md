# Overview

Chong was a state said to predate the Shang dynasty in traditional historiography. The earliest alleged attestation lies with Gun 鯀, an Earl 伯 who was tasked with controlling a great flood. It was later attested to be an ally of the Shang dynasty, defending it from attack. 

The location of Chong is thought to be the Laoniupo 老牛坡 site in Shaanxi 陕西.

# History

Chong as a state was heavily tied to the Taotang and Youyu clans from the Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors period. During a time of severe flooding, Gun, the Earl of Chong, was enlisted to try and control the situation. He laboured for almost a decade, but to no avail, resulting in his exile to Feather Mountain, where he died. 

Chong would not appear again until the Shang dynasty. There are two possible Marquesses, Hu and Bao, who were military allies of King Wu Ding, though this is disputed. A second Marquess Hu is attested in Records of the Grand Historian as an ally of Di Xin during the dynasty's final years. During King Wen of Zhou's reign of Predynastic Zhou, he laid siege to Chong, leading to its surrender and absorption into Zhou territory as Chengyi 程邑.
