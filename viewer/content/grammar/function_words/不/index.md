## Explanation

不 negates verbs and descriptions in Literary Chinese. It is placed before the verb or description.

{% corpus_quote path="中國漢文/clean/宋朝/南宋/三字經/三子經.txt" text="苟不教，性乃遷，教之道，貴以專。" highlight="不" source="三字經" %}
{% corpus_quote path="中國漢文/clean/周朝/東周/戰國時代/周/詩經/大雅/文王之什/靈臺/靈臺.txt" text="經始靈臺，經之營之，庶民攻之，不日成之。" highlight="不" source="詩經·大雅·文王之什·靈臺" %}
{% corpus_quote path="中國漢文/clean/周朝/東周/戰國時代/周/詩經/大雅/文王之什/皇矣/皇矣.txt" text="帝謂文王、予懷明德、不大聲以色、不長夏以革。" highlight="不" source="詩經·大雅·文王之什·皇矣" %}

When negating a verb, 不 + Verb may include a pronoun between the two, forming 不 + Pronoun + Verb.

{% corpus_quote path="中國漢文/clean/周朝/春秋時代/魯/論語/先進.txt" text="居則曰：『不吾知也。』如或知爾，則何以哉？" highlight="不吾" source="論語·先進" %}

Here, 吾 is the object of 知: “they do not know me.”

The he/her/they equivalent, 之, can also be used:

{% corpus_quote path="中國漢文/clean/漢朝/東漢/論衡/論衡__juan_68.txt" text="宅者長幼所共，加慎致意者，何可不之諱？" highlight="不之" source="論衡·四諱六十八卷" %}

However, care should be taken to ensure a verb appears after: It has a verb sense in "to go," which can also be used in the same structure.

## Technical explanation

不 is the general negative particle used before verbal and stative predicates. In constructions such as 不吾知, a pronominal object may be placed before the verb when the clause is negated.

With respect to literary readings, 不 is typically pronounced as 否 with a high tone; the pronunciation the word has today originally came from 弗.

## Comparisons

* 非 - Essentially 不 but for nouns.
* 否 - A hard "no" omitting the verb.
* 無 - To "not have".
* 弗 - Essentially a variant, but more often implies the object pronoun 之.
