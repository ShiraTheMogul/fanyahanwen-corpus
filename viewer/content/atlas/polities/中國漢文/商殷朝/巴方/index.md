## Overview

Bafang (巴方) was a polity represented in the corpus’s Shang-period folderisation.

The research inventory dates or describes it as: Xia dynasty, Shang dynasty.

## Geography

Sichuan Province, also argued to be to be southern Shaanxi or Jianghan

## Relationship with Shang

The inventory classifies it as an enemy of Shang.

## Attestation

The research inventory lists the following forms of attestation:

- Bamboo Annals
- Oracle bones
