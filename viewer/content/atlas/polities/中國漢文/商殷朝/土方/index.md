## Overview

Tufang (土方) was a polity represented in the corpus’s Shang-period folderisation.

The research inventory dates or describes it as: Early to middle Shang dynasty, conquered by Wu Ding.

## Geography

Northwest of Yin, east of Hong Fang (present Fen River Basin, Shanxi Province)

## Relationship with Shang

The inventory classifies it as an enemy of Shang.

## Attestation

The research inventory lists the following forms of attestation:

- Book of Poetry (Chang Fa (長發)
- Oracle bones
