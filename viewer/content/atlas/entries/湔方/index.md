## Overview

Jianfang (湔方) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Jianfang appears in the surviving sources as an enemy of Shang.

## Geography

It was located west of Yin (Shanxi-Shaanxi Plateau).
