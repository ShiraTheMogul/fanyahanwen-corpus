## Overview

Renfang (人方) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Renfang appears in the surviving sources as an enemy of Shang.

## Geography

It was located southeast of Yin, on the coast of the Donghai (present-day Huai River basin, Shandong Peninsula, northern Anhui, and southern Shandong).
