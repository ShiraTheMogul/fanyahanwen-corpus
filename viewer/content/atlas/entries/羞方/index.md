## Overview

Xiufang (羞方) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Relations between Xiufang and Shang appear to have changed over time.

## Geography

It was located west of Yin (Shanxi-Shaanxi Plateau).
