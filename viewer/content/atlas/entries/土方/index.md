## Overview

Tufang (土方) was a polity during the early to middle Shang dynasty, mentioned in the ‘Chang Fa’ (長發) ode of the *Book of Poetry*, and attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Tufang appears in the sources as an enemy of Shang and was conquered during the reign of King Wu Ding.

## Geography

It was located northwest of Yin, east of Hong Fang (present-day Fen River Basin, Shanxi Province).
