## Overview

Bafang (巴方) was a polity associated in the sources with the Xia and Shang periods, mentioned in the *Bamboo Annals*, and attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Bafang appears in the surviving sources as an enemy of Shang.

## Geography

It is usually associated with Sichuan, although southern Shaanxi and the Jianghan region have also been proposed.
