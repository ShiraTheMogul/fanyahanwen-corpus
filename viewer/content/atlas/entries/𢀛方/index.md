## Overview

Gongfang (𢀛方) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, continuing into the reign of Zu Geng. It is attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Relations between Gongfang and Shang appear to have changed over time.

## Geography

It was located northwest of Yin and west of Tufang, in the northern part of present-day Shaanxi on the Shanxi–Shaanxi Plateau.
