## Overview

Zhou (周方) was a polity from the middle to the late Shang dynasty, mentioned in the *Records of the Grand Historian*.

## History

Relations between Zhou and Shang changed over time. Zhou ultimately overthrew King Di Xin and established the Zhou dynasty.

## Geography

It was located west of Yin (present-day Qishan County).
