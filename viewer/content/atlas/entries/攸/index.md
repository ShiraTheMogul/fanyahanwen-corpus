## Overview

You (攸) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

The surviving sources suggest that You was allied with Shang.

## Geography

It was located east of Yin (present-day southern part of Yongcheng City and northwestern part of Suzhou City).
