## Overview

The Kingdom of Dongning (東寧國) was a Ming loyalist colonial state located in southern Taiwan, founded in resistance to the encroaching Qing dynasty. It was founded by Zheng Chenggong 鄭成功 in Anping 安平 (modern-day Tainan 臺南), and would exist for around half a century before finally being wrestled down by the Qing. It got its name from Zheng Jing 鄭經, the second ruler of the dynasty.

## History

Zheng Chenggong wrestled control of southern Taiwan from the Dutch on 2nd February 1662, who was known to them as Koxinga (國姓爺). This was done through piracy over the seas in junks 䑸, taking advantage of the Dutch's distraction with the islands of Penghu 澎湖. The Siege of Zeelandia 熱蘭遮城包圍戰 secured Zheng Chenggong's control, allowing the predominantly Han Chinese migrants to colonise the island. Bows, muskets, and other weapons led to a total defeat, and a peace treaty was signed that led to Zheng Chenggong gaining control. This was, however, whilst the native Kingdom of Middag 米達赫王國, who had been colonised by the Dutch before, desperately attempted to fend them both off, but to no avail. The Middag people were severely exploited by the Kingdom of Dongning and would never see full control of the island of Taiwan again. The Zheng clan's rule would be marked by taking the Hua-Yi distinction of the classics to heart and attempting to "Sinicize" and "civilise" the remaining Dutch and Middag people.

By 1666, the Kingdom of Dongning became a self-sustaining colony, with Chen Yonghua 陳永華 cultivating sugar cane to trade with Europeans and evaporating salt to increase its quality. Grain plantations were plentiful, and farms reached 43,000 hectares. Connections with Japan flourished and produced coinage for the kingdom.

Shi Lang 施琅, at one time a defector to the kingdom, had a falling out with Zheng Chenggong and defected back to the Qing. Over time, he became an essential commander in defeating the Zheng clan, first driving them off the mainland and then slowly but surely whittled away at their Taiwan-based stronghold through repeated military victories. The third and last ruler, Zheng Keshuang 鄭克塽, eventually surrendered. The surrender went smoothly, with the leaders having their heads shaved Manchu-style and the Ming calendar abolished. The 17 remaining Ming princes were allowed to live in mainland China for the rest of their lives. The House of Koxinga was given a hereditary title of She 舍.

## Culture

The Kingdom of Dongning was chiefly an agricultural nation, with the military spending time farming whenever they were not engaged in military action. Constant attacks in the Taiwan strait and from the Kingdom of Middag meant time was limited until 1666 when stability was achieved. Within the poetry tradition, themes of grief and longing for the return of the Ming dynasty.

## Use of Literary Chinese

The Kingdom of Dongning maintained a population of Ming princes and scholars, who would take part in the Literary Chinese tradition. Annals, poetry, and essays are all extant and preserved by authorities from the Republic of China.
