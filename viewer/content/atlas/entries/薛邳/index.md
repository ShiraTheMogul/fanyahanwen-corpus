## Overview

Xue or Pi (薛／邳) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, mentioned in the *Records of the Grand Historian*, and attested in bronze inscriptions.

## History

The surviving sources suggest that Xue or Pi was allied with Shang.

## Geography

It was located east of Yin (present-day Tengzhou).
