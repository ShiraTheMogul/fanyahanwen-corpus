## Overview

Dapeng (大彭) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, mentioned in the *Records of the Grand Historian* and the *Bamboo Annals*, and attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Relations between Dapeng and Shang appear to have changed over time.

## Geography

It was located east of Yin (present-day Tongshan).
