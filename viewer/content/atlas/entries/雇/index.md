## Overview

Gu (雇) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

The surviving sources suggest that Gu was allied with Shang.

## Geography

It was located south of Yin (present-day southwest of Yuanyang County, northwest of Yuanwu Town, Henan Province).
