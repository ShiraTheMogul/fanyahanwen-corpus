## Overview

Qiang (羌方) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, mentioned in the *Records of the Grand Historian* and the *Book of Poetry*, and attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Relations between Qiang and Shang appear to have changed over time.

## Geography

It was located northwest of Yin, across parts of present-day Shaanxi and Gansu on the Shanxi–Shaanxi Plateau.
