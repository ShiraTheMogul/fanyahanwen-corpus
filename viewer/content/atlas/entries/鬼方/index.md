## Overview

Guifang (鬼方) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, mentioned in the *Records of the Grand Historian* and the *Bamboo Annals*.

## History

The surviving sources suggest that Guifang was allied with Shang.

## Geography

It was located northwest of Yin, in present-day central Shanxi and northern Shaanxi on the Shanxi–Shaanxi Plateau.
