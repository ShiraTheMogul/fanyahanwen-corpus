## Overview

Zhifang (沚方) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Relations between Zhifang and Shang appear to have changed over time.

## Geography

It is usually placed west of Yin on the Shanxi–Shaanxi Plateau, although an eastern location has also been proposed.
