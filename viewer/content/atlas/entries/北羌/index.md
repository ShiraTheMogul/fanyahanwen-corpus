## Overview

Northern Qiang (北羌) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Relations between Northern Qiang and Shang appear to have changed over time.

## Geography

It was located northwest of Yin, north of Qiangfang.
