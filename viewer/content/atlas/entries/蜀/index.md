## Overview

Shu (蜀) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, mentioned in the *Bamboo Annals*, and attested in oracle-bone inscriptions.

## History

Shu appears in the surviving sources as an enemy of Shang.

## Geography

It was located in Sichuan.
