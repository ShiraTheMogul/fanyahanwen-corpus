## Overview

Hufang (虎方) was a polity during the Shang dynasty, mentioned in the *Records of the Grand Historian*, and attested in bronze inscriptions.

## History

Relations between Hufang and Shang appear to have changed over time.

## Geography

It was located east of Yin.
